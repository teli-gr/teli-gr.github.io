How much road tax will I pay for 2027?

Work out your 2027 Greek road tax (τέλη κυκλοφορίας) for a car or motorbike from your registration certificate: year of first registration (box B), engine size (P.1) or CO₂ emissions (V.7).

Pay by 31/12/2026. After that: +25% in January, +50% in February, +100% from 1 March (minimum surcharge €30). If you won't drive the vehicle, declare it off the road on myCAR by 31/12/2026.

What if…

I sold the vehicle. Who pays the road tax?

The transfer can't be completed until the road tax for that year is paid, so the seller usually pays it. If the transfer is completed in the new year, the seller pays the new year's tax too. gocar.gr (in Greek)

I don't have a TAXISnet login. How do I find my notice?

AADE has a page that works without a login: enter the owner's tax number (AFM) and the number plate, choose the year and print the notice. The link is just under the pay button. AADE: road tax without TAXISnet (in Greek)

I have a disability. Am I exempt?

For certain types of disability, yes: for one car owned 100% by the person with the disability (or by the parent of a minor), up to 1,650 cc. For complete paralysis or loss of both legs, the limit is 2,650 cc at 80% disability or more and 3,650 cc at 100%. Apply at your tax office (ΔΟΥ) or online through "My requests" (Τα αιτήματά μου) on myAADE, with your KEPA disability certificate. AADE: road tax for people with disabilities (in Greek)

My vehicle doesn't show up on myCAR.

Contact your local tax office (ΔΟΥ) and ask them to add the vehicle to your tax number (AFM). AADE myCAR guide (in Greek)

The vehicle was stolen or scrapped.

You can't declare these online as off the road. Send a request to your tax office (ΔΟΥ) or to AADE's help centre (ΚΕΦΟΔΕ) through "My requests" (Τα αιτήματά μου) on myAADE. AADE myCAR guide (in Greek)

We own the vehicle together. Who pays?

It's enough for one of the co-owners to pay. If it isn't paid, each owner is liable for the full amount. To take it off the road, all owners must agree. gocar.gr · AADE (in Greek)

I paid, but it still shows as unpaid.

Keep your receipt until AADE's records update. If they don't, contact AADE and show them the receipt. larissanet.gr (in Greek)

I want to put the vehicle back on the road.

Ask to lift the off-the-road status (άρση ακινησίας) on myCAR. You need to pay road tax for the time you'll drive it and have valid insurance. AADE myCAR guide (in Greek)
